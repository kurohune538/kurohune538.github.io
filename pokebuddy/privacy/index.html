端末・ブラウザ・診断情報

ブラウザ版の保存領域・通信ログ

広告に関する情報（ネイティブアプリで広告を有効にした場合）

3. 利用目的

  • 法令、ストア規約および利用条件への対応

アプリのアンインストール、ブラウザ保存領域の削除、またはログアウトだけでは、サーバー上のアカウントは削除されません。 手続きは アカウント削除ページ をご覧ください。

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the handling of information in the PokeBuddy iOS and Android apps and the browser-based web app (together, the “Services”), provided by PokeBuddy developer (Yamico) (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

  • An anonymous or registered account user ID.
  • Email address and authentication provider information.
  • Display name and profile image supplied when linking Google or Apple.

Content and activity you provide

  • Decks, card lists, deck version history, collections, adjustment cards, and related metadata.
  • Optional details you record for each owned card, such as condition, finish, grading information, acquisition price and currency, acquisition date, personal valuation and its currency, and notes.
  • Match results, opponent deck types, notes, battle logs, and usage history.
  • Replay data automatically sent from successfully completed two-deck solo games played without free moves or Undo, including card-ID deck lists, initial setup, actions and choices, outcome, end reason, turn count, random seed, rules/card-data version, and Service version.
  • Service preferences, notification read status, and card-data reports.
  • Profile images or other images you explicitly choose to upload.

Solo-game replay data does not include deck names, display names, profile information, human-readable battle logs, or device information. It is stored with an internal user ID only to enforce submission limits, delete the data when the account is deleted, and match bug reports you send with the games they refer to.

Device, browser, and diagnostic information

  • Device or browser type, operating system, Service version, language, and network type.
  • Country or approximate region inferred from an IP address.
  • When those features are enabled in a native app, Firebase installation identifiers (for Analytics), and crash and error data, performance data, network URLs, and response timing processed by Sentry.
  • Push notification token and platform when notifications are enabled.

Browser storage and access logs

  • The web app stores sign-in sessions, the return path used after OAuth, preferences, cross-tab sign-out synchronization, and caches used to display card data and other content more quickly in browser storage such as local storage, session storage, and IndexedDB.
  • When you access the web app, the hosting infrastructure may process access logs such as IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, browser and device information, access time, requested URL, and response status.

We use this browser storage to provide and secure the Services, remember preferences, synchronize state, and display content. We do not use it for advertising tracking. The web app does not enable Google Analytics for Firebase, Sentry, or Google AdMob.

Advertising information (when ads are enabled in a native app)

  • Advertising ID, App Set ID, IP address, product interactions, and diagnostic information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Advertising consent status and non-personalized advertising preference.

Camera and photos

The Services access the camera or photo library only when you use a relevant feature, such as scanning a deck code, importing cards or decks, or choosing an image. Import images are generally processed on your device. A profile image or similar content may be uploaded to cloud storage only after you choose to save it.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide authentication, synchronization, backup, and core functionality of the Services.
  • Provide deck, match, collection, CPU battle, and notification features.
  • Investigate possible improvements to AI battle features using automatically sent solo-game replay data that meets our collection criteria. We do not adopt a change based on this data alone; safety and performance are checked in separate evaluation battles.
  • Investigate bug reports you send by replaying your solo-game replay data.
  • Display ads, manage consent, measure advertising, and prevent fraud when advertising is enabled in a native app.
  • Diagnose crashes, improve performance, secure the Services, and provide support.
  • Comply with applicable laws, store requirements, and our terms.
  • Providing matchup tables from anonymized match records. We aggregate deck types, opponent deck types, results, and BO1 / BO3 format in a way that does not identify individuals. A deck matchup is shown only when it has at least 5 games from at least 3 users, and individual records, user IDs, and display names are never shown.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

We use the following service providers where necessary to operate the Services:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and realtime data.
  • Google Firebase (native apps only) for Google Analytics for Firebase (aggregate usage) and Cloud Messaging (push notifications).
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., United States; native apps only) for crash, error, and performance reporting.
  • Google AdMob (when ads are enabled in a native app) for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and consent management.
  • Vercel (web app) for web hosting, content delivery, access-log processing, and security.
  • Apple and Google for sign-in and app distribution.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Advertising SDKs may process or share identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to your consent and device settings. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer.

5. Retention and Deletion

Account information and user-provided content are generally retained while your account remains active. After we verify a deletion request, we generally delete or de-identify associated data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Data in backups and service-provider systems is deleted or de-identified according to each provider’s deletion cycle, applicable contracts, security needs, and legal obligations, so completion times may vary.

Uninstalling an app, clearing browser storage, or signing out does not delete server-side account data. See the Account Deletion page to submit a request.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Manage notification, camera, and photo permissions in device settings.
  • Clear information stored by the Services in your browser through your browser settings. This does not delete your server-side account or synchronized data.
  • Manage advertising choices through the consent interface and device ad settings.
  • Delete decks, matches, and other supported content within the Services.
  • Request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your information.
  • You can choose in the Services’ settings not to include your match records in the anonymized matchup aggregation. The change applies from the next aggregation.
  • Replay data from two-deck solo games that meets our collection criteria is sent automatically after the game is completed and cannot be disabled in Settings. Previously stored replay data can be deleted by deleting your account or requesting restriction of processing.
